Privacy

This public privacy page describes the current LinkUp website experience. It will be expanded and finalized as the LinkUp product and its data practices are brought into production.

Information submitted to LinkUp

Information submitted through Contact & Reports may include your name, email address, category, subject and message. This information is intended for support, safety and product feedback operations.

Installation

The LinkUp website can be installed as a web application on supported browsers. Browser installation behavior is controlled by the browser and device.

Future product services

The main LinkUp app will have additional account, messaging, media and social functionality. Its final privacy policy will describe those services before they are released.

Questions

Use the official LinkUp website to contact the team with privacy questions.
